GIGGLE DX - SOUND EFFECTS
=========================

How to add one: import the sound into the Sounds folder and give it EXACTLY the name in the
left column. The game looks sounds up by name (scripts/sfx), so nothing else needs changing.
A sound that isn't imported yet is just skipped, so the game never breaks.

[HAVE]  already imported
[NEED]  not imported yet
(demo)  plays in the Southern Tower / dungeon demo


PICKING THINGS UP AND FANFARES
------------------------------
[HAVE] snd_fanfare_item      (demo)  Big item fanfare: the grapple hook, the boss key, any sword,
                                     shield, armor, gloves, flippers, boots, bomb bag, quiver
[HAVE] snd_fanfare_bun       (demo)  Getting a piece of the Bun (end of a dungeon, the big one)
[HAVE] snd_heart_container   (demo)  Getting a heart container (from the boss)
[HAVE] snd_item_get          (demo)  Small item jingle: map, compass, small keys, money from a
                                     chest, refills, bottles
[HAVE] snd_chest             (demo)  A chest opening (just before the item rises out)
[HAVE] snd_key               (demo)  A small key appearing when a room is cleared
[HAVE] snd_heart             (demo)  Picking up a heart
