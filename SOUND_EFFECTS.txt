GIGGLE DX - SOUND EFFECTS
=========================

How to add one: import the sound into the Sounds folder and give it EXACTLY the name in the
left column. The game looks sounds up by name (scripts/sfx), so nothing else needs changing.
A sound that isn't imported yet is just skipped, so the game never breaks.

[HAVE]  already imported
[NEED]  not imported yet
(demo)  plays in the Southern Tower / dungeon demo
